Controls

Player 1 (Red)

  • A / D — Move
  • W — Jump
  • S — Crouch / Fast-fall
  • F — Light Slash
  • G — Heavy Slash
  • Space — Dodge / Dash
  • Left Shift — Block (hold) / Parry (tap)

Player 2 (Blue)

  • ← / → — Move
  • ↑ — Jump
  • ↓ — Crouch / Fast-fall
  • K — Light Slash
  • L — Heavy Slash
  • / — Dodge / Dash
  • Right Shift — Block (hold) / Parry (tap)

Tap block the instant before a hit lands to parry — it fully negates damage and leaves the attacker stunned. Holding block absorbs most damage but drains stamina and lets some chip damage through. Dodging grants brief invincibility but costs stamina and has a cooldown, so don't spam it.
